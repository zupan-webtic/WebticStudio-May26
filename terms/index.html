3. Services provided

Webtic d.o.o. provides the following services under the name Webtic Studio:

Brand audits
A structured diagnostic review of an existing brand. We assess your current visual identity, messaging, positioning, and digital presence against your market, competitors, and growth stage. Deliverables include a written assessment of gaps, inconsistencies, and strategic opportunities, as a report and/or presentation.
Brand strategy & positioning
Strategic work covering market positioning, target audience definition, competitive differentiation, and brand architecture. We work with founding teams and senior leadership to establish direction that informs subsequent creative work. Typical outputs include a positioning document, messaging hierarchy, and brand narrative framework.
Visual identity systems
Design and development of a complete visual identity — logomark, typography, colour system, iconography, and usage guidelines — for consistent application across digital, print, and physical environments. Deliverables include master files in agreed formats and a brand guidelines document.
Website design & development
End-to-end design and development of marketing websites: information architecture, UX, visual design, copy direction, front-end development, and launch support. Sites are built to perform across devices. Handover includes source files, CMS access where applicable, and technical documentation.
Messaging & narrative
Development of verbal identity — brand voice guidelines, core messaging pillars, taglines, homepage copy, and pitch narrative — delivered as a written document with application examples across key touchpoints.
